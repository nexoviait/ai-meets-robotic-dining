# Ai Meets Robotic Dining

Build this app for AI HUB BD. The attached images are screenshots and concept visualisations of the desired screens and robotic kiosk stations (Coffee & Tea Bar, Automated Snack Station, Automated Lunch & Dinner Station, and Automated Food-Service Station), accompanied by the full Investor Memorandum and Pitch Deck. Make a modern landing page and full site, making sure all are standard, premium, and fully functional.

Note on Google Stitch URLs: The user's external download links point to private Google Accounts authenticated endpoints; everything needed for the complete brand identity, UI, menu structure, pricing, hub layout, and features is provided directly in the attached screenshots, pitch deck, and investor memorandum.

## Locked
- Brand & Identity: "AI HUB BD" with the signature tagline "Idea → AI → Product → Automation → Business". Bangladesh's first physical AI innovation hub (8,000 sq ft flagship in Dhaka, 200+ AI-enabled workstations, 3,000 seat-hours daily capacity, BDT 20 Cr launch plan) connected to a scalable digital platform and automated robotic food-service café.
- Visual Language & Aesthetics: Ultra-modern, high-tech, premium dark theme with glowing neon purple (#8B5CF6 / #A855F7), electric cyan (#06B6D4 / #38BDF8), robotic warm amber/gold (#F59E0B), sleek brushed steel counters, glass reflection effects, clean sans-serif typography (Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter), and responsive cards.
